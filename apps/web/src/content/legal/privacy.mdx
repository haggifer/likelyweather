## 1. What information do we collect?

### Personal information you disclose to us

**_In Short:_** _We do not ask you for personal information._

Forecast Arena has no accounts, sign-up, forms or uploads. You can use every part of it without telling us who you are.

**City search.** When you search for a city, our server sends the text you type to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching places, and keeps the results for 24 hours so the same search is answered without asking again. The search text is not linked to you or to your IP address.

**Tracked cities.** When you open a city for the first time, it joins our list of cities (its name, region, coordinates and time zone), and we fetch its forecast whenever it is opened and a newer one exists. The list is not shown to other visitors. It records cities, not people: nothing in it identifies who opened a city, how often or when.

**Your country.** On your first visit, the four "Start with" cities are the largest ones in your country. Our hosting provider's content delivery network works out the country from your IP address and passes only its two-letter code to our server, which uses it to pick those cities and neither stores nor logs it.

**Your location.** Only when you press "Use my location", and only if your browser allows it, the website reads your position once and sends it to our server rounded to two decimals (about one kilometre) to find the nearest stored city. The position is not stored or logged, and the website never asks for it on its own; you can withdraw the permission in your browser at any time.

**The Android app.** The app sends our server the same requests as the website, and everything above applies to it. "Use my location" works as described for the website, except that Android asks for the permission (location while the app is in use, never in the background), and that when no stored city is within 50 km the app asks Android's own geocoder on your phone for the name of your town and then searches for that name with our server, as a typed search would. The home-screen widget asks our server for the forecast of the one city you chose for it, about once every three hours; each such request is the same as opening that city in the app.

**Sensitive Information.** We do not process sensitive information.

### Information automatically collected

**_In Short:_** _Your IP address is used in short-lived counters that limit how many requests one visitor can make._

- _Request limits._ Each request to our server comes with your IP address. We count the requests from each address over short time windows (1 minute for reading forecasts and for searches, 1 hour for opening new cities), and the requests that make our server fetch new data from the weather services for you, which are limited per address per day. Each count is stored with the address (for an IPv6 address, only its first half, which identifies your network or device rather than the full address) and deleted automatically 1 minute or 1 hour after the last request it counts (so at most a minute or an hour after its window ends), or at 00:00 UTC for the daily limit. We do not use the address for anything else, and our logs do not record it.
- _Delivering the website._ Like any website, ours receives your IP address and browser information with each request, because our hosting provider needs them to deliver the pages to you. We do not keep access logs. When a request fails, we log the page or API path it asked for, without your IP address or search text, and keep that log for 14 days.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## 5. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

**_In Short:_** _We store only the language, the color theme and the places you choose, and the places never leave your browser._

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not allow third parties to place tracking technologies on our Services. We store:

- **NEXT_LOCALE** (a cookie): remembers the language you choose. It is set only when you switch the language, and expires after one year.
- **fa.mode** (in your browser's local storage, not sent to us): remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. It is set only when you switch the theme, and stays until you clear your browser's site data.
- **fa.places** (in your browser's local storage, not sent to us): remembers your favorite places and up to 50 places you last picked in the city search, which the website shows as shortcuts. It is set when you pick a place in the search, add or remove a favorite, open the nearest city with "Use my location", or on your first visit, when the website fills the shortcuts with the largest cities of your country; it stays until you clear your browser's site data.
- **fa.appPromo.dismissedAt** (in your browser's local storage, not sent to us): the moment you closed the "Get the app" bar on an Android phone, so it stays closed for 30 days. It is set only when you close the bar.
- **fa.quota** (in your browser's local storage, not sent to us): the last answer our server gave about your daily request count, so the meter in the top bar shows it at once when a page opens. It holds only the limit, the count and the reset time, is replaced on every answer, and is ignored once the reset time has passed.

The Android app keeps on your phone, in its own storage and never sent to us: your favorite and recent places, your language and theme choices, the city each home-screen widget shows with the numbers it last displayed, and the answers it last received from our server, kept for up to one day so they stay readable offline. Those answers include the forecasts, your request count, the starter cities, and your recent searches with what you typed and their results. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android's settings deletes all of it.

Because these only keep the website working as you use it, we do not ask for your consent to them. You can delete or block them in your browser settings; the website then uses its default language, your system's color theme and its starter cities.
